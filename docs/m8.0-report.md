## Frozen contract

The binding contract is [M8 evidence-bound disposition contract](m8-disposition-contract.md).
It defines:

- typed, allowlisted investigation proposals rather than free-form executable
  Agent actions;
- server-rebuilt evidence and target bindings to completed authorized runs;
- maintenance, quality, and bounded material-discrepancy reference cases;
- explicit evidence states that distinguish supported investigation,
  suspected mismatch, inconclusive evidence, and independently confirmed
  physical agreement or disagreement;
- a draft, submission, independent-review, expiry, cancellation, and immutable
  terminal-state lifecycle;
- M8 capabilities, role extensions, tenant/factory scope, resource hiding, and
  reviewer/initiator separation of duties;
- idempotency, optimistic concurrency, immutable submitted content, and
  append-only reviews;
- sanitized transition and authorization audit requirements;
- 30 mandatory security and regression cases; and
- a strict prohibition on MES/ERP/PLC execution or autonomous approval.

The “reported A, used B” idea is retained as a material-discrepancy reference
case. It does not replace the M8 roadmap and is not represented as an
implemented or dataset-validated capability.
