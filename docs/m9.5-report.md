## Implemented scope

M9.5 adds:

- creator-only submission from `simulated` to `pending_approval`;
- mandatory current completed-simulation and snapshot binding checks;
- snapshot rebuild and fail-closed drift invalidation before submission and
  review;
- `submit:action_plans` for creators and `review:action_plans` for supervisors
  and admins;
- enforced creator/reviewer separation of duties;
- append-only approved/rejected review records with bounded rationale;
- terminal `plan_approved` and `plan_rejected` states;
- creator cancellation from draft, validated, or simulated state;
- server-clock expiry for nonterminal plans;
- optimistic versions and scoped idempotency for every explicit transition;
  and
- sanitized append-only transition audits.

## Lifecycle evidence

The PostgreSQL suite covers valid submit/approve and submit/reject flows,
creator self-review denial, idempotent retries, stale/incompatible transitions,
terminal-state rejection, cancellation, expiry, drift before submission,
drift before review, immutable source/binding/manufacturing content, and the
continued absence of an execute route.

Approval changes only the M9 plan record and append-only review/audit history.
Protected source proposals, reviews, evidence, plan bindings, operational
manufacturing state, and dataset records remain unchanged.

## Explicit boundary

`plan_approved` is a human acceptance record, not execution authorization.
M9.5 does not create maintenance/quality tickets, send notifications, export
external-system payloads, change inventory or genealogy, hold/release
production, contact equipment, actuate PLCs, or write MES/ERP/QMS/WMS state.
